Aditya Mehta

I'm an undergraduate student studying Computer Science at Caltech.

I'm currently a researcher in Georgia Gkioxari's lab, working on advancing perception for large vision-language models. Previously, I was at the Caltech Vision Lab, where I developed methods adapting vision transformers for unsupervised and semi-supervised segmentation of microscopy images. My interests broadly revolve around machine learning and computer vision, especially large vision-language models and advanced recognition tasks.
